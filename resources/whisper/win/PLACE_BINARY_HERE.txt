Optional - only needed for the microphone / voice-input button.

AUTOMATIC:  Setup-Windows.bat -WithVoice

MANUAL:
  1. From https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/releases download
     whisper-bin-x64.zip (if the newest release has no files attached, use the
     newest one that does). Copy EVERYTHING inside its Release\ folder
     (whisper-server.exe plus all the .dll files) into this folder.
  2. Download a model, e.g.
       https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp/resolve/main/ggml-base.en.bin
     and save it here as:  model.bin

Result:
  resources\whisper\win\whisper-server.exe
  resources\whisper\win\model.bin
  resources\whisper\win\*.dll

Without these, the mic button just reports "unavailable" - everything else
in the app works fine.
